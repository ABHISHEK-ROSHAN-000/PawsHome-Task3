Before you apply

Ask the questions that protect the animal and the adopter.

Housing permission, time alone, exercise, veterinary costs, travel plans, children, other animals, and emergency care should all be discussed before placement.

01

Can the home support the animal?

Check space, routine, finances, tenancy rules, family agreement, and long-term plans.

02

Does the animal fit the home?

Review temperament, handling comfort, energy, health, and compatibility.

03

Is support available after adoption?

Plan veterinary care, training, adjustment time, and a contact for follow-up questions.
