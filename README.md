# PawsHome

## What is PawsHome?

PawsHome is a demonstration website about animal adoption in India. It is built as a concept — a thoughtful example of how a responsible adoption platform could look and work.

It brings everything related to pet adoption into one simple journey: meeting adoptable pets, understanding what makes a good match, learning how to help, reporting an animal in need, and preparing a home before a pet arrives.

Every animal, story, and form on the site is fictional and clearly labelled as a demonstration. The animal-welfare facts and figures shown on the site are real and come from recognised research sources.

## Why it exists

Right now, an estimated 69.3 million cats and dogs in India live without secure homes. At the same time, many adoptions happen too quickly, based only on a photograph or an impulse, which can leave both the animal and the adopter in a difficult situation.

PawsHome explores a calmer, more careful path: helping people ask better questions, understand a pet's personality and health, prepare their home properly, and make adoption a lifelong decision rather than a quick one.

## What you'll find on the site

- **Adopt** – Sample pet profiles that go beyond a photo. Each profile explains the pet's personality, health, energy, and the kind of home that would suit them, so people can look for a real fit, not just a nice photograph.
- **Stories** – Typical rescue-to-home journeys that show how adoption realistically works, from immediate safety to a stable, loving home.
- **Get involved** – The many ways to help even without adopting: fostering a pet for a while, volunteering (feeding, transport, photography, events, and more), and how to understand where support genuinely goes.
- **Pet care** – Friendly, practical guidance for new pet parents: the first days at home, vaccination, keeping children and pets safe, and building a daily routine that works for everyone.
- **Report a rescue** – A sample form that models how urgent animal reports should be structured. It does not send data anywhere; for an animal in real danger, visitors are directed to local clinics, municipal services, and official grievance bodies.
- **About** – How real research is used and clearly labelled, so no one mistakes the fictional parts for genuine rescue cases.

## A note on what PawsHome is not

PawsHome is a concept and demonstration project. It is not an active rescue service, shelter, adoption matching service, or donation platform. The pets shown are fictional characters using licensed stock photography, and the forms do not send or store any information.

If an animal is in real, immediate danger, always contact a local veterinary clinic, a recognised local rescue team, or the official municipal or state authorities.
