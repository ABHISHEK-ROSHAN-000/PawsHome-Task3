About PawsHome

A fictional platform built around real research.

PawsHome was created as an AI-assisted internship project. The organisation, profiles, forms, and donation menu are fictional; the wider animal-welfare data and educational sources are real and linked.

The idea

Bring adoption, fostering, rescue reporting, and pet-care education into one clear journey.

Many animal-welfare websites must serve very different visitors at once. The PawsHome structure separates urgent reporting, long-term adoption, volunteer pathways, and general education so each user can find the right next step.

  • 01Profiles explain fit, not only appearance.
  • 02Forms collect the context rescuers and adoption teams need.
  • 03Research disclosures prevent fictional achievements from being presented as real.

Research and transparency

Every real claim has a visible source.

The project uses primary or recognised sector sources where possible and clearly labels sample profiles, composite stories, and illustrative costs.
