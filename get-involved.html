Get involved

Give time, space, transport, or a useful skill.

Animal welfare depends on more than rescuers. Foster homes, drivers, photographers, event helpers, caregivers, veterinary partners, and communicators all strengthen adoption outcomes.

Volunteer roles

Choose work you can sustain.

The best role is not the most dramatic one. It is the role you can perform reliably, safely, and under clear supervision.

Field support

Feeding and shelter care

Assist with food preparation, cleaning, enrichment, observation, and record keeping under a trained coordinator.

Logistics

Transport support

Help move animals, supplies, carriers, and volunteers while following safe-handling and hygiene instructions.

Adoption

Events and follow-up

Welcome visitors, explain the process, document applications, and support post-adoption communication.

Creative

Photography and profiles

Create accurate, respectful photographs and profile text without exaggerating temperament or health.

Digital

Awareness and research

Prepare educational content, verify sources, update listings, and explain responsible adoption clearly.

Professional

Veterinary and training partners

Qualified professionals can support assessment, vaccination, treatment, behaviour, and caregiver education.

Foster care

A temporary home can create the evidence an adopter needs.

Foster caregivers help animals recover, learn routines, and reveal behaviour that may not be visible in a shelter. They also provide accurate notes for matching.

  • 01Confirm household permission, quarantine needs, and compatibility with resident animals.
  • 02Record eating, toileting, sleep, handling, exercise, and behaviour changes.
  • 03Follow veterinary and rescue instructions; do not independently rehome the animal.

Donation transparency

A credible platform should explain what money supports.

PawsHome does not accept payments. This section demonstrates how a real organisation could show purpose, limits, receipts, and reporting before asking for support.

Transparency checklist

Before donating, look for proof.

  • Registered organisation details
  • Clear purpose and beneficiary
  • Receipts or invoices
  • Privacy and refund terms
  • Progress updates
  • Official payment account
